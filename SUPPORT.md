# Getting help

- **Questions and story talk:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/NebulanEnt/mistvale-rpg/discussions)
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/NebulanEnt/mistvale-rpg/issues/new/choose) and fill in the form
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

Crashes are much easier to fix when you attach `logs/latest.log` (and the crash report from `crash-reports/`, if one exists).
