# Code of Conduct

Mistvale is meant to be a cozy place. Everyone who takes part in this repository, its issues and its discussions is expected to help keep it that way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Minecraft modding.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you disagree instead of getting sharper.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam or advertising.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discrimination or hateful language of any kind
- Sexual or violent content, or unwanted attention
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find hostile in a community like this

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated problems lead to a permanent ban from the repository.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or discussion in question. For anything that needs to stay private, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/NebulanEnt/mistvale-rpg/security/advisories/new) and mention that it is a conduct report.
